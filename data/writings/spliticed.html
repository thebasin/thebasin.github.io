In a phenomenal bout of good
luck, I saw it—whirring
& spinning, the clock atop
the mantel—freeze in place
like dry ice; it became a
pale shade of blue with
tiny diamonds crystallizing
its surface. A wonder the
room didn’t just crack
right in half, break
& splinter, disintegrate
to dust then & there. Alas,
as quickly as the blueness &
its adornment of jewelry
appeared, it all melted
right away. Perhaps it
was a trick of the light,
my vision getting in the
way of my own eyes.
The little hand started
up first, smoothly traversing
its way around the circle,
shortening & elongating
as if it were a needle
making its way to the middle
of a vinyl. It hummed, softly.
At the first tick of the big
hand, a phantasmal
feeling of heat filled the
room, a dry heat. I could barely
breathe—my skin began to
sag, forming a thick putty
which, melt melt melted me away.
The hands spinning by in such a
blur that I could barely see my eyes.

whistles and spurts of nacreous
flow, spitting & stopping, f(it)ting
& breaking, spl(it)iced, freezing
over on t(him)—a cartographer’s
dream, a disjunctive beam—
connective gestation & machinic
infatuation. It uttered this
proclamation: I ddam here, two
eyes have, in a phenomenal bout
of...

heatwave. Nanospasmic meltdown... all
cylinders fired, running makes it hotter.
Slippery surfaces slide everything
right off, a meltdown, reaching
zero at the speed of light & only going
faster.
