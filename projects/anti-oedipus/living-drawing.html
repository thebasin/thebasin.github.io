Change its course.

An abstract field of production, branching, and conjunction, drawn in moving ink and pigment.

Drag the colored forms to change their connections. Tap the three endpoints on the right to open or close a branch. Sweep across a current to interrupt it; it slowly reconnects. Drag the empty field to stir it.

With a keyboard
Focus the drawing, then use ← / → to select a form and W A S D to move it. Enter opens or closes a selected branch. Space pauses; R starts again.

Movement begins paused if you prefer reduced motion. You can resume it whenever you like.
