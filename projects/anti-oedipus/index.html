HOW TO READ THIS MAP

A return is a change
of register.

Hardt describes concepts becoming articulated through successive returns: machinery, critique of the syntheses, then social formations. This map extends that reading aid through your notes on schizoanalytic practice.

The three syntheses coexist. The spiral is a way of reading their returns, not a timeline of three successive psychological stages. Likewise, the three social machines privilege different syntheses without exclusively possessing one.

Term colors indicate the primary part used to index each concept: sage for I, terracotta for II, blue for III, and plum for IV. Concepts can return across several parts; their detail panels show those returns. Spatial proximity helps navigation, but the named connections carry the argument. The master map has no enclosed chapter territories.

Solid connections carry a named conceptual relation. Dashed connections mark a return in the spiral. “Appears in your notes” supplies source context; co-occurrence alone is never presented as a causal connection.
