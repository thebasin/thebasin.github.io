# Anti-Oedipus
## Part I — The Desiring-Machines

### §1 — Desiring-Production

#### The Three Modes of Production

- Everything is a factory/machinic production, with three simultaneous modes:
	
	- **1. Production of production — [[Ideas/Concepts/Connective synthesis|connective synthesis]]: “and…and…and…”**
		
		- Machines connect to machines, producing and cutting [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
			
		- This productive process produces the **[[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|Body without Organs]] (BwO)**: an unproductive, undifferentiated full surface.
			
		- The BwO is antiproductive and initially opposes production.
			
		- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Paranoiac machine|Paranoiac machine]]:** [[Ideas/Concepts/Repulsion|repulsion]] whereby the BwO repels [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|desiring-machines]] as invasive/persecutory.
			
		- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Miraculating machine|Miraculating machine]]:** [[Ideas/Concepts/Attraction|attraction]] whereby the productions of desiring-machines are appropriated and recorded on the BwO as though they emanated from it.
			
	- **2. [[Ideas/Concepts/Production of recording|Production of recording]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Disjunctive synthesis|disjunctive synthesis]]: “either…or…or…”**
		
		- Positions, differences, and intensities are distributed across the BwO.
			
		- In its legitimate/inclusive use, terms remain distinct without one becoming the exclusive identity that cancels the others.
			
	- **3. Production of consumption/[[Ideas/Concepts/Consummation|consummation]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Conjunctive synthesis|conjunctive synthesis]]: “so it’s me…”**
		
		- [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|Intensive states]] are consummated and a subject emerges afterward as a **residue of the process**.
			
- **Schizophrenic use of the syntheses:** immanent and nonexclusive; connections remain productive, disjunctions inclusive/mobile, and the subject passes through intensive states without being fixed to one identity.
	

#### Concrete Example of the Three Syntheses

- **Connective:** breast-machine **AND** mouth-machine **AND** swallowing-machine…
	
- **Disjunctive:** breast-zone **OR** mouth/breast connection **OR** milk-flow **OR** pleasure-intensity… These are only examples from a much larger multiplicity distributed across the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]].
	
- **Conjunctive:** pleasure, dizziness, satiation, frustration, etc. are consummated/passed through → subject finds itself: **“so it’s…me!”**
	
- The subject is thus a residuum of the productive process rather than its origin.
	

#### Milk Example

- Mouth-machine connects with breast-machine; this connection produces/draws and simultaneously [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]] the milk-flow.
	
- At the same time:
	
	- **Production:** mouth-machine ↔ breast-machine ↔ milk-flow.
		
	- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Production of recording|Recording]]:** connections, differences, and intensities produced by the event are inscribed/distributed across the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]]—a “carving in immanence” conditioning further production.
		
	- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Consummation|Consummation]]:** pleasure, satisfaction, frustration, satiation, etc. are lived as [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]].
		
- From these intensive states the [[Ideas/Concepts/Residual subject|residual subject]] emerges: **“so, it’s me.”**
	

### §2 — The Body without Organs

#### BwO

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]]:** full surface/plane on which things flow, connect, and become distributed.
	
- It can be understood as immanent/virtually limitless; its [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]], intensities, and striations situate its organization.
	
- Production and [[Ideas/Concepts/Antiproduction|antiproduction]] coexist within the assemblage through **[[Ideas/Concepts/Repulsion|repulsion]] and [[Ideas/Concepts/Attraction|attraction]]**.
	
- Their varying differential relation produces [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]].
	
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Celibate machine|Celibate machine]]:** the new arrangement in which those intensities are consummated.
	

#### BwO and Socius

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|Socius]]:** basically the social [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]] insofar as the full body becomes a surface of social [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]] and appropriation.
	
- This does **not** mean socius = [[Ideas/Concepts/Social machine|social machine]]; that distinction matters in Part III.
	

#### Football-Field Example

- Football field = [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]]/full surface on which production happens.
	
- **Production:** snap **AND** block **AND** throw **AND** catch **AND** tackle…
	
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Production of recording|Recording]]:** distribution of the differences produced by the play:
	
	- ball is here rather than there;
		
	- bodies occupy new positions;
		
	- turf is torn;
		
	- fatigue changes;
		
	- score/down changes;
		
	- tactical possibilities open/close.
		
- These differences can be taken up by larger recording surfaces—statistics, coaching decisions, standings, contracts, media—and condition subsequent production.
	
- Recording is not merely memory; it is the **real [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]/distribution of differences produced by the event**.
	

### §3 — The Subject and Enjoyment

- The subject does not preexist productive processes; it is produced as a **residuum alongside the [[Ideas/Concepts/Celibate machine|celibate machine]]**.
	
- The differential relation between [[Ideas/Concepts/Attraction|attraction]]/[[Ideas/Concepts/Repulsion|repulsion]] produces intensive quantities/states, consummated in the celibate machine.
	
- The subject passes through and consumes/consummates these states, finding itself in each: **“so it’s me!”**
	
- Order: **productive process → intensive state → [[Ideas/Concepts/Consummation|consummation]] → [[Ideas/Concepts/Residual subject|residual subject]].**
	
- The lived intensive state comes first; the subject experiencing it emerges residually from that state.
	
- The subject is therefore decentered, without permanent fixed identity, and continually produced through the [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]] it passes through.
	

#### Eternal Return and the Subject

- The subject itself is not what repeats; **the production of subjectivity repeats**.
	
- The desiring/[[Ideas/Concepts/Celibate machine|celibate machine]] occupies the abandoned center, while subjects repeatedly emerge around it as the residual **“so it’s me!”**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Eternal Return|Eternal Return]] = repetition of this subject-producing process **through difference**, not persistence of one identical subject through changing states.
	

### §4 — A Materialist Psychiatry

#### Desire, Lack, and Need

- Desire does **not** originate in [[Ideas/Concepts/Lack|lack]]; desire is the productive machinic coupling itself—machines connecting, producing, and cutting [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
	
- Hunger and food [[Ideas/Concepts/Need|need]] not follow **hunger → lack of food → desire for food**.
	
- Hunger, eating, food, pleasure, and satiation can all be components of one [[Ideas/Concepts/Productive assemblage|productive assemblage]].
	
- Becoming full can terminate the eating-connection without implying that production began because food was absent.
	

##### Apple Example

- An apple may actually be present or absent, but its absence does not produce desire.
	
- If absent, [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] still operates through real relations: hunger, memories, images, signs, movement, money, expectations, environments.
	
- Within that assemblage, **“I [[Ideas/Concepts/Lack|lack]] an apple”** can itself be produced.
	
- **Lack is therefore a secondary countereffect within desiring-production, not its origin.**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Need|Need]] is similarly secondary: it appears when productive relations are organized around deprivation, interruption, or loss of their objective conditions.
	
- The subject only afterward emerges residually: **“I desire the apple.”**
	

#### Desiring-Production and Social Production

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|Social production]] is not desire projected onto society;** it is [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] itself operating under determinate historical conditions.
	
- The [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]] organizes, records, channels, and captures [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
	
- Desire remains primary; [[Ideas/Concepts/Lack|lack]]/[[Ideas/Concepts/Need|need]] are counterproducts produced when the productive field is socially organized, blocked, appropriated, or made dependent upon particular conditions.
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Antiproduction|Antiproduction]] is associated with the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]]’s resistance to production.
	
- The [[Ideas/Concepts/Paranoiac machine|paranoiac machine]] is specifically the repulsive relation whereby the BwO repels [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|desiring-machines]].
	
- Thus **antiproduction ≠ simply the paranoiac machine**, although the paranoiac machine expresses one relation of antiproduction.
	

##### Socially Produced Needs

- Food/housing may physically exist, but if access is organized through money, **“I [[Ideas/Concepts/Need|need]] money”** becomes a real need.
	
- The need is real, but it did not exist prior to the social system making money the necessary mediator.
	

##### Desire and Fascism

- Oppressive social arrangements are not simply imposed on passive people from outside.
	
- Desire can itself become invested in hierarchy, authority, identity, discipline, segregation, repression, etc.
	
- D&G’s question is therefore not merely **“Who deceived people?”** but **how did desire itself come to invest and reproduce this arrangement?**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Molar formations|Molar]] organization is not inherently fascist.
	
- Fascism is a particular [[Ideas/Concepts/Libidinal investment|libidinal investment]] in which desire reproduces repression, hierarchy, segregation, and ultimately destructive/abolitionist tendencies.
	

#### Technical Machines and Desiring-Machines

- **Regime = mode of functioning.**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Technical machines|Technical machines]] generally function when intact and distinguish machine from product.
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|Desiring-machines]] produce through [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]], interruptions, breakdowns, and connections.
	
- Their “malfunction” is part of their functioning, and the product continually feeds back into production.
	

#### Forms of the Social Machine

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Primitive territorial machine|Primitive territorial machine]]:** codes [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
	
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic machine|Despotic machine]]:** retains codes but subordinates/overcodes them through a higher code.
	
- **Capitalism:** decodes old flows, deterritorializes them, lets decoded flows circulate, then controls them through axioms—especially money.
	

#### Coding, Overcoding, Decoding, and Deterritorialization

- Capitalism decodes [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] and deterritorializes the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]].
	
- In doing so, it pushes [[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|social production]] toward its schizophrenic limit: increasingly free [[Ideas/Concepts/Molecular relations|molecular]] flows.
	
- The overcoming of older territorial codes also creates the possibility of increasingly rigid paranoiac [[Ideas/Concepts/Molar formations|molar formations]].
	
- The [[Ideas/Concepts/Primitive territorial machine|primitive territorial machine]] produces **hysteria** where coded bodily flows break down.
	
- These flows may then be reorganized through:
	
	- **despotic [[Ideas/Concepts/Overcoding|overcoding]]:** forces them back into a higher ordering structure;
		
	- **capitalist [[Ideas/Concepts/Decoding|decoding]]:** frees them from the territorial code.
		

### §5 — Machines, Flows, and the Three Cuts

#### Machines and Flows

- Every machine is simultaneously:
	
	- a **cut in one flow**;
		
	- relative to another machine, the **producer of another flow**.
		
- Example: **stomach-machine → intestine-machine → anus-machine**
	
	- intestine [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]] the stomach’s flow;
		
	- produces the shit-flow;
		
	- anus cuts that flow.
		

#### The “Third Machine”

- [[Ideas/Concepts/The third machine|The third machine]] matters because no machine is an absolute source or final receiver.
	
- Every coupling opens onto another coupling: **and…and…and…**
	
- The “continuous” or “infinite” flow is relational/ideal, not literally an endless quantity of matter.
	
- An actual material flow can stop, harden, decay, disappear, etc.
	
- There are no completely self-sufficient objects behind the machines.
	
- Things are machinic insofar as they enter relations of production, flow, interruption, and connection.
	

#### The Three Kinds of Cut

- **1. [[Ideas/Concepts/Flow-cut|Flow-cut]] / withdrawal from a flow — [[Ideas/Concepts/Connective synthesis|connective synthesis]]: “and…and…and…”**
	
	- A machine draws off/intercepts part of a continuous flow.
		
	- Example: mouth-machine [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]]/draws from milk-flow produced by breast-machine.
		
	- **Function:** produces connections and [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
		
- **2. [[Ideas/Concepts/Chain-detachment — schizz|Chain-detachment]] / schizz — [[Ideas/Concepts/Disjunctive synthesis|disjunctive synthesis]]: “either…or…or…”**
	
	- Something becomes detachable from a chain of [[Ideas/Concepts/Production of recording|recording]] and capable of entering other combinations.
		
	- A sign, organ, gesture, or fragment can detach from one chain and recombine elsewhere rather than remain fixed to one meaning/function.
		
	- **Function:** records/distributes differences without fixing them into one exclusive identity.
		
- **3. [[Ideas/Concepts/Residual break|Residual break]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Conjunctive synthesis|conjunctive synthesis]]: “so it’s me…”**
	
	- After production and recording, a remainder falls to the subject.
		
	- Subject emerges as the residue of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]] being consummated.
		
	- Pleasure, dizziness, satiation, etc. are passed through and from each state a subject appears again: **“so it’s me.”**
		
	- **Function:** produces the nomadic/[[Ideas/Concepts/Residual subject|residual subject]].
		

## Part II — Psychoanalysis and Familialism: The Holy Family

### Chapter 2 — Three Texts of Freud

#### Group Fantasy vs. “Individual” Fantasy

- For D&G, fantasy is fundamentally **social rather than private**.
	
- Desire directly invests the real social field; parents can transmit/relay these investments but are not their ultimate source.
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]] takes dispersed social relations and represents them through the private triangle **daddy → mommy → me**.
	
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Group fantasy|Group fantasy]]:**
	
	- directly invested in real articulations of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]];
		
	- institutions experienced as contingent and therefore capable of being destroyed, transformed, rearranged;
		
	- its “subject” is the plurality of drives/[[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|desiring-machines]] rather than a fixed ego.
		
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/So-called individual fantasy|So-called individual fantasy]]:**
	
	- same social field apprehended through imaginary personal representations;
		
	- institutions appear stable/“immortal”;
		
	- ego identifies with assigned positions and exclusive identities;
		
	- Oedipus/castration are exemplary forms of this [[Ideas/Concepts/Familialization|familialization]].
		
- Hence there is ultimately no genuinely individual fantasy: instead **[[Ideas/Concepts/Subject-groups|subject-groups]]** and **[[Ideas/Concepts/Subjugated groups|subjugated groups]]**.
	
- Oedipal fantasy is one way members of a subjugated group imaginatively experience their real social membership.
	
- Either type of group can shift into the other.
	

#### Freud’s Beating-Fantasy

- Freud takes **“a child is being beaten”** and reconstructs it as an individual Oedipal drama.
	
- D&G instead treat it as a **collective machinic assemblage**: teacher, children, punishment, sexuality, institutional discipline, parental fragments, social authority, etc.
	
- Positions such as boy/girl, punished/observer, father/teacher are mobile positions within this machinery rather than expressions of one hidden familial identity.
	

##### Core Reversal

- Psychoanalysis: teacher = father, colonel = father, authority = father.
	
- D&G reverse the direction: familial figures are **local relays within a much larger social field**.
	
- Psychoanalysis takes social [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] and folds it backward into the Oedipal theater.
	

### Chapter 3 — The Connective Synthesis of Production

- Legitimate [[Ideas/Concepts/Connective synthesis|connective synthesis]]: **“and…and…and…”**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|Desiring-machines]] form partial, open, nonspecific connections.
	
- Psychoanalysis totalizes these beneath Oedipal wholes—mother, father, child, phallus, castration.
	
- Instead of allowing partial connections to remain immanent/nonspecific, psychoanalysis extrapolates them into complete familial objects.
	

### Chapter 4 — The Disjunctive Synthesis of Recording

- Legitimate [[Ideas/Concepts/Disjunctive synthesis|disjunctive synthesis]] is inclusive: **“either…or…or…”**
	
- Different positions remain mobile and can coexist.
	
- Psychoanalysis transforms this into an **exclusive either/or** organized around [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]]:
	
	- either the subject explicitly identifies with/lives through the Oedipal structure at the imaginary level;
		
	- or the subject’s differences/deviations are interpreted as effects of that same Oedipal structure.
		
- In either case, **Oedipus wins**.
	
- There is no genuine outside because even resistance to Oedipus is interpreted through Oedipus.
	
- The Oedipal disjunction is transcendent/repressive because it fixes difference into predetermined alternatives rather than allowing inclusive/mobile disjunctions.
	

### Chapter 5 — The Conjunctive Synthesis

- The [[Ideas/Concepts/Conjunctive synthesis|conjunctive synthesis]] should allow the [[Ideas/Concepts/Residual subject|residual subject]] to pass nomadically through many social-historical [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]]: **“so it’s me.”**
	
- Psychoanalysis instead **biunivocalizes** those states, mapping the social field onto **daddy ↔ mommy ↔ me**.
	
- **“So it’s me” → “So it was your father.”**
	
- Parents are only local inductors within a desiring/social process that exceeds them.
	

#### Becoming and Proper Names

- Names such as Woman, Mongol, Joan of Arc, Louis XVII, etc. do not designate identities the ego copies.
	
- They designate **intensive zones/effects on the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]] through which the subject passes**.
	
- Subject emerges afterward as the residue of these passages: **“so it’s me.”**
	
- Becoming = **passage through an intensive determination**, not identification with a person.
	

#### Oedipus and the Social Field

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]] is an [[Ideas/Concepts/Application|application]] of the social field onto the family, not the family projected outward onto society.
	
- Desire already invests social machines, institutions, class relations, race, nation, religion, etc.
	
- Family = local relay through which these larger relations are reduced to **daddy–mommy–me**.
	

#### Segregative Use of the Conjunctive Synthesis

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Segregative use|Segregative use]] fixes **“so it’s me” → “so I’m one of us.”**
	
- Subject becomes stabilized through belonging to race, nation, religion, class, group, etc., usually against some “them.”
	

#### Nomadic / Polyvocal Use

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Nomadic use|Nomadic use]] does not fix the subject permanently in one zone.
	
- Subject passes through racial, historical, sexual, cultural, social, etc. zones of intensity and temporarily finds itself within them before continuing.
	
- This grounds the distinction between **racist and racial poles**:
	
	- racist/paranoiac pole → fixes and segregates identities;
		
	- schizonomadic pole → passes through racial and other intensive zones.
		
- A temporary **“so it’s us”** can still occur; the question is whether it hardens into an exclusive identity or remains open to further becoming.
	

#### Desire vs. Conscious Interest

- Desire and [[Ideas/Concepts/Conscious interest|conscious interest]] are not identical.
	
- Someone can consciously/politically oppose a social order while remaining libidinally invested in its machines, habits, pleasures, institutions, conveniences, hierarchies, etc.
	
- A conjunctive **“so it’s me”** never exhausts the entire unconscious productive field.
	
- Hence D&G reject **ideology** as the primary explanation of oppression:
	
	- people are not simply fooled into supporting oppressive arrangements;
		
	- the [[Ideas/Concepts/Social machine|social machine]] itself can be desired;
		
	- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Desire belongs to the infrastructure|desire belongs to the infrastructure]].**
		
- Segregative/paranoiac and schizonomadic poles are tendencies, not permanent kinds of people.
	
- The unconscious can oscillate between fixation, movement, segregation, and becoming.
	

#### Schizoanalytic Method

- Schizoanalysis asks:
	
	- What machines does desire plug into?
		
	- Where does it become fixed?
		
	- Where does it move?
		
	- How does desire invest the social field?
		
	- How can desire come to desire its own repression?
		
- Same logic applies to texts: don’t primarily ask **“What does this secretly mean?”** Ask what it does, what it connects to, what intensities it produces, and what new production it makes possible.
	

### Chapter 6 — Psychic and Social Repression

#### Social Repression vs. Psychic Repression

- Desire is revolutionary insofar as [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] can disrupt established social organization.
	
- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Social repression|Social repression]] comes first; [[Ideas/Concepts/Psychic repression|psychic repression]] is its inward delegation.**
	
- Direction: **social repression → psychic repression → Oedipalized desire.**
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]] is not the original thing repressed; it is the familial form desire assumes **under repression**.
	

##### Double Operation

- Social formation delegates repression to the family.
	
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Psychic repression|Psychic repression]] disguises what was actually repressed—[[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]]—as Oedipal/incestuous desire.
	
- Remaining question: how to distinguish desire that opens connections from desire that invests its own repression.
	

### Chapter 7 — Neurosis and Psychosis

#### Freud’s Distinction

- Freud:
	
	- **neurosis:** preserves reality by repressing desire;
		
	- **psychosis:** lets desire break through at the cost of reality.
		
- D&G reject this distinction as overly Oedipal.
	
- Both are effects of how [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] becomes organized/interrupted.
	

#### Neurosis

- Oedipal imprint more or less “takes.”
	
- Desire becomes trapped inside the family triangle and circulates within it.
	

#### Psychosis

- Oedipal imprint does not take in the same way.
	
- Desire exceeds the familial frame and begins to invest broader social, historical, political, and cosmic fields.
	
- Psychosis should not simply be understood as a “loss of reality.”
	
- D&G ask whether the apparent loss of reality may partly be produced by forcing non-Oedipal desire back into Oedipal organization.
	

### Chapter 9 — The Process

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Schizophrenia as process|Schizophrenia as process]]:** [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] at the deterritorialized limit of [[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|social production]].
	
- Passage through decoded [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]], [[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive states|intensive states]], and [[Ideas/Concepts/Deterritorialization|deterritorialization]].
	
- Clinical psychosis is **not identical** to this process.
	
- Clinical breakdown can constitute an interruption, blockage, or collapse of the process.
	
- Madness may contain moments of breakthrough, but D&G do not simply identify breakdown itself with liberation.
	

### Five Paralogisms of Psychoanalysis

- These are **not five separate chapters**; they function as a conceptual index to the illegitimate uses of the syntheses developed across Part II.
	
- **1. [[Ideas/Concepts/Extrapolation|Extrapolation]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Connective synthesis|connective synthesis]]**
	
	- Psychoanalysis takes partial, nonspecific connections and extrapolates a global complete object/signifier—[[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]], phallus, [[Ideas/Concepts/Lack|lack]].
		
- **2. [[Ideas/Concepts/Double bind|Double bind]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Disjunctive synthesis|disjunctive synthesis]]**
	
	- Inclusive **“either…or…or…”** becomes exclusive **this or that—never both**.
		
	- Whatever happens, subject is forced back into Oedipal coordinates.
		
- **3. [[Ideas/Concepts/Application|Application]] — [[Ideas/Concepts/Conjunctive synthesis|conjunctive synthesis]]**
	
	- Social, political, racial, historical investments are mapped biunivocally onto the family: **“so it was your father/mother.”**
		
- **4. Displacement / disfiguration of the repressed**
	
	- Psychoanalysis mistakes Oedipal desire for what was originally repressed.
		
	- For D&G, **[[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] is what is repressed**; Oedipus is the distorted image desire assumes under repression.
		
- **5. [[Ideas/Concepts/The afterward|The afterward]]**
	
	- Psychoanalysis treats present social/material conditions as secondary reactivations of an earlier Oedipal conflict.
		
	- D&G reverse it: **actual desiring-production is primary → Oedipus is reactive/derived.**
		

## Part III — Savages, Barbarians, Civilized Men

### §1 — The Inscribing Socius

#### Social Production and Coding

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|Desiring-production]] exists wherever [[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|social production]] exists.
	
- Precapitalist societies do not suppress desire from outside; their social machines **code the [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] of desire from within**.
	
- Fundamental problem of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]]: **How do we prevent flows from escaping the code?**
	
- Capitalism is historically exceptional because it operates through **decoded flows** rather than intrinsic codes.
	

#### Universal History

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Universal history|Universal history]] is retrospective, contingent, and nonteleological.
	
- Capitalism lets us look backward and recognize earlier societies as social machines organized against decoded [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]].
	
- Capitalism was never destined to occur; it arose through contingent encounters, ruptures, and conjunctions.
	

#### Socius vs. Social Machine

- **[[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|Socius]] = full body / surface of social [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]].**
	
- In the [[Ideas/Concepts/Primitive territorial machine|primitive territorial machine]]: **Earth = socius.**
	
- Production is recorded/distributed upon the Earth, which appears as the **[[Ideas/Concepts/Quasi-cause|quasi-cause]]** from which production seems to emanate.
	
- This repeats the miraculating logic of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]] at the social level.
	
- But **socius ≠ [[Ideas/Concepts/Social machine|social machine]]**:
	
	- socius = [[Ideas/Concepts/Production of recording|recording]] surface/full body;
		
	- social machine = operative organization that codes [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] around that body.
		
- Therefore:
	
	- **Earth = primitive socius / full body**
		
	- **primitive territorial machine = social machine**
		

#### Social Machines vs. Technical Machines

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Technical machines|Technical machines]] perform particular operations.
	
- Social machines organize people, technical machines, production, reproduction, and social positions into a broader regime.
	
- The same object can function on both levels.
	
- Example: a clock technically measures time but socially organizes canonical hours/coordinates labor.
	

#### Coding Flows

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Coding flows|Coding flows]] = making production socially determinate by selecting [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]], cutting chains, distributing tasks, assigning shares, and assigning positions.
	
- Primitive imperative: **Nothing must escape coding.**
	

#### Inscription Before Exchange

- Primitive [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]] is primarily about **[[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]**, not exchange.
	
- Society first determines who/what may circulate, how circulation occurs, and under what conditions.
	
- Bodies/organs are marked and distributed before exchange takes place.
	
- Exchange is therefore secondary to coding.
	

#### Collective Investment of Organs

- Organs are not naturally treated as private possessions.
	
- Their uses—seeing, speaking, sexuality, eating, reproduction, labor—are socially coded/distributed.
	
- Capitalism increasingly produces a **disinvestment/[[Ideas/Concepts/Privatization of organs|privatization of organs]]**, contributing to the production of the modern “private individual.”

#### Cruelty

- Cruelty = primitive territorial regime of **direct bodily [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]**.
	
- Examples: tattooing, scarification, initiation, punishment.
	
- It does not mean primitive societies simply possess a natural enjoyment of violence.
	
- It means culture **materially writes its codes onto bodies**, producing durable social memory.
	

#### Sign and Desire

- The sign is a **position of desire**.
	
- Marks do not merely represent social relations; they actively plug bodies, organs, and desire into [[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|social production]].
	
- “Writing” = [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]] broadly, not merely alphabetic writing.
	
- The social field is already writing on bodies before literal systems of writing appear.

### ch 2: The Primitive Territorial Machine
- Earth = full body / [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]]: production is recorded and coded on the body of the earth.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Primitive territorial machine|Primitive territorial machine]]: codes [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] through the declension of [[Ideas/Concepts/Filiation|filiation]] and [[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|alliance]].

- Filiation is primary in [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]: lineage, descent, fixed stock.
- Alliance is secondary but irreducible: it connects filiative groups laterally through marriage/exogamy and mobile blocks of debt.

- Debt and exchange: debt belongs principally to alliance and directs how flows move between filiative stocks. Exchange/circulation happens within this already-coded system, rather than founding it.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Surplus value of code|Surplus value of code]] = the primitive form of surplus value: detachments from chains and selections from flows create functional imbalances—excess, deficiency, prestige, rank, obligations—which are redistributed through the coded social field. It is not yet capitalist monetary surplus value.
- Segmentation is central: the machine continually [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]] and rearranges lineages and alliances.

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Scission|Scission]] = a segment breaks away and forms a new lineage.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Fusion|Fusion]] = separate segments unite through opposition to another group.

- The machine therefore works by breaking down: blockages, rivalries, ruptures, scissions, and recombinations are how it reproduces itself.
- The primitive machine uses scission to prevent excessive fusion/centralized power, but also fears scission going so far that coding collapses into decoded flows.
- Capitalism appears retrospectively as the opposite tendency: generalized [[Ideas/Concepts/Decoding and deterritorialization|decoding and deterritorialization]] of flows; later these flows will be organized through an axiomatic rather than territorial codes.

###  ch 3. The Problem of Oedipus

- Intensive/germinal [[Ideas/Concepts/Filiation|filiation]]: unextended, prepersonal filiation; distinctions of person, sex, and generation are not yet fixed. Disjunction is inclusive and signs are ambiguous (+ and −).
- Twinness / Dogon egg: image of this intensive state—different positions can coexist in the same being rather than belonging to discrete persons.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|Alliance]] = second characteristic of [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]: secondary to filiation but not derived from it. Alliance produces an extensive system containing both lateral alliances and extended filiations. 
- Somatic order: the extensive order of distinct bodies, sexes, generations, and kinship positions. Intensive ambiguity becomes exclusive/restrictive disjunction: this rather than that.
- +/− system: in extension, signs become determinate—gained/lost, owed/received, passing/blocked. D&G turn Lévi-Strauss’s logical kinship system into a material system of [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] and debts. 
- Germinal → somatic: the extensive system is produced from intensive conditions but then represses them; intensive filiation survives mainly in myth.
- Incest is impossible: before extension there are no determinate “mother” or “sister” persons; after extension those persons exist only as already prohibited partners. Incest therefore cannot be the original content of desire. 
- What is actually repressed is the intensive germinal / noncoded flow of desire. Incest is the distorted image imposed on it by the extensive system.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]] = [[Ideas/Concepts/Displaced limit|displaced limit]]: the real limit is decoded desire escaping social coding; Oedipus/incest relocates that threat inside the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]] as a manageable familial image.

[[Ideas/Concepts/Three instances of representation|Three instances of representation]]

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Repressed representative|Repressed representative]]: intensive germinal influx / decoded desire—the real object of repression.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Repressing representation|Repressing representation]]: alliance and extensive social coding, which determine persons and what may pass or remain blocked.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Displaced represented|Displaced represented]]: incest/Oedipus—the false image produced when repression says, effectively, the decoded desire you wanted was incest. 
- Part II connection: this is the fourth paralogism (displacement/disfiguration of the repressed) in more concrete terms: [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-production|desiring-production]] is actually repressed, while Oedipus is substituted for it and treated as though it were the thing desire originally wanted.

Spine:  
[[Ideas/Concepts/Intensive germinal filiation|intensive germinal filiation]] → alliance/extension → somatic distinctions + [[Ideas/Concepts/Determinate signs|determinate signs]] → repression of intensive flow → [[Ideas/Concepts/Incest as displaced image|incest as displaced image]] → Oedipus as displaced limit.

### ch 4: Psychoanalysis and Ethnology

- Real limit = decoded [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] of desire.

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Absolute limit|Absolute limit]]: schizophrenia as fully decoded/deterritorialized flows.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Relative limit|Relative limit]]: capitalism, which decodes flows but immediately axiomatizes and reterritorializes them.
- Capitalism therefore approaches the absolute limit while continually pushing it away.

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]] = displaced/internalized limit.

- What all societies really fear is decoded desire.
- Oedipus displaces that danger into a manageable familial form.
- It can exist as a limit without being occupied as an actual complex.
- Under capitalism it becomes occupied/lived because the social field is folded onto the private family. 

- Conditions of Oedipalization

- social reproduction separates from familial reproduction;
- detachable fragments become a transcendent detached object (phallus);
- this object performs a folding/reduction (rabattement) of the social field onto the familial field;
- Oedipus moves from [[Ideas/Concepts/Displaced represented|displaced represented]] to representative of desire. 

- Primitive societies are not secretly Oedipal.

- Family, [[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|alliance]], [[Ideas/Concepts/Filiation|filiation]], ritual, politics, economics, etc. remain directly continuous with the social field.
- Oedipus is therefore not naturally the hidden truth of these relations.

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Schizoanalytic method|Schizoanalytic method]]: replace “What does it mean?” with “How does it work? What does it do?”

- Symbols are not primarily representations of hidden meanings.
- They function within machines, flows, [[Ideas/Concepts/Cuts|cuts]], connections, and practices.
- The unconscious does not represent; it produces. 

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Molar formations|Molar]] / [[Ideas/Concepts/Molecular relations|molecular]]

- Molar: large determinate formations—family, economy, politics, institutions, identities.
- Molecular: [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|desiring-machines]], [[Ideas/Concepts/Partial objects|partial objects]], flows, connections.
- At the molecular level, use = functioning = production = formation.
- Desiring-machines produce the very connections through which they function. 

- Desire directly invests the social field.

- Social formations are not expressions of hidden familial meanings.
- Molecular desire invests and underdetermines the molar formations it composes.
- Hence a microphysical/micrological unconscious and microinvestments beneath conscious political/economic interests. 

- Sexuality/desire at the molecular level

- libido connects partial objects;
- inclusive disjunctions organize relations on the [[Ideas/Concepts/Body without Organs|BwO]];
- zones of intensity are produced;
- the [[Ideas/Concepts/Residual subject|residual subject]] emerges afterward.

Spine:  
decoded flows → social limit → Oedipus displaces/internalizes that limit → capitalism occupies Oedipus by folding the social onto the family → schizoanalysis rejects interpretation and studies how molecular desiring-machines actually function within molar social formations.

### ch 5: Territorial Representation

- Society is inscriptive rather than fundamentally exchangist: debt follows directly from [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]; exchange/circulation is secondary to the forces that mark and organize the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]]. 
- D&G reject the exchangist picture of a closed, balanced system. Primitive alliances form an open system of debts characterized by [[Ideas/Concepts/Functional disequilibrium|functional disequilibrium]], which produces [[Ideas/Concepts/Surplus value of code|surplus value of code]]. 
- Primitive graphism is independent of speech, not writing subordinated to speech. Territorial signs remain heterogeneous and polyvocal.
- Magic/savage triangle:  
	[[Ideas/Concepts/Voice–audition|voice–audition]] ([[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|alliance]]) → [[Ideas/Concepts/Graphism–body|graphism–body]] ([[Ideas/Concepts/Filiation|filiation]]) → [[Ideas/Concepts/Eye–pain|eye–pain]] (evaluation/surplus value).  
	The sign does not merely represent something; inscription acts directly on the body and produces social reality/desire. 
- This is the theater of cruelty: bodily marking creates [[Ideas/Concepts/Collective memory|collective memory]] and the debtor–creditor relation. Debt is therefore not reducible to exchange. 
- Bad debtor/criminal: the voice–body connection has failed to “take”; punishment re-establishes the inscription, while the eye extracts surplus value of code from the pain. 
- Territorial representation forms a complicated network connecting voices, bodies, marks, affects, debts, and [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]], without reducing them to a single meaning or signifier. 
- It can code pain and death, but cannot foresee/code the form of its own destruction from outside; this is the transition to the [[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic machine|despotic machine]]. 



### ch 5: Territorial Representation

- Representation varies with the [[Ideas/Concepts/Social machine|social machine]].

- There are still the three in-depth elements from the previous chapter: [[Ideas/Concepts/Repressed representative|repressed representative]] / [[Ideas/Concepts/Repressing representation|repressing representation]] / [[Ideas/Concepts/Displaced represented|displaced represented]].
- But how repression operates changes historically.
- Primitive territorial representation actually has a relatively high affinity between social machines and [[Ideas/Concepts/Desiring-machines|desiring-machines]]: desire remains polyvocal and has not yet been trapped in the later representational/Oedipal form. 

- The [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]] is inscriptive, not fundamentally exchangist.

- The primitive system is about marking/coding bodies and [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]], not primarily exchanging equivalents.
- Debt results directly from [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]].
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|Alliance]] codes flows and creates a social memory of words; through debt, alliance is articulated with [[Ideas/Concepts/Extended filiation|extended filiation]], repressing the intensive/germinal filiative influx. 

- Against the exchangist/structural model — five basic objections:

- Alliance is not produced by extended filiation; lateral alliance/debt instead conditions extended filiations.
- Kinship is a physical system of intensities and flows, not merely a logical combinatorics.
- Disequilibrium is fundamental and functional, not a later deviation from an original equilibrium.
- The system is open, especially through debts, alliances, generations, and [[Ideas/Concepts/Surplus value of code|surplus value of code]], rather than a closed cycle of exchange.
- Social reproduction cannot be reduced to circulation/exchange; beneath circulation are the productive, political forces of inscription. 

- Territorial signs / graphism

- Primitive societies are oral not because they [[Ideas/Concepts/Lack|lack]] graphism, but because graphism remains independent of the voice.
- Voice and graphism are heterogeneous but coordinated.
- The sign does not merely represent something: its inscription acts on the body and produces a position of desire. 

- Magic/savage triangle — this is not a linear sequence, but three relatively autonomous sides:

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Voice–audition|voice–audition]]: voice of alliance
- hand–graphics: inscription on the body of extended filiation
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Eye–pain|eye–pain]]: the eye apprehends/evaluates the relation between voice and marked body, extracting from the pain a surplus value of code
- D&G summarize it as action of the voice of alliance / passion of the body of [[Ideas/Concepts/Filiation|filiation]] / reaction of the evaluating eye.
- Together these form the theater of cruelty and the surface organization of territorial representation. 

- Nietzsche / debt / memory

- Cruel inscription produces a [[Ideas/Concepts/Collective memory|collective memory]] and makes people capable of alliance and the debtor–creditor relation.
- Primitive debt consists of open, mobile, [[Ideas/Concepts/Finite blocks of debt|finite blocks of debt]], not exchange equivalents. 
- The bad debtor is someone for whom the mark has failed to “take”: the gap between voice/alliance and marked body/filiation has widened.
- Punishment re-establishes that relation through pain, while the eye extracts surplus value of code. 

- Death of the territorial system

- Territorial representation can code pain, crime, and death within its system.
- What it cannot anticipate is the form of its own destruction from outside.
- The State arrives as a rupture: old territorial codes are destroyed or subordinated, and the old finite debts will be absorbed into [[Ideas/Concepts/Infinite debt|infinite debt]]. That is the transition to §6. 


 spine:

variable [[Ideas/Concepts/Social repression|social repression]] → inscription rather than exchange → alliance/debt conditions extended filiation → five critiques of exchange → independent voice + graphism → magic triangle / cruelty → debt creates memory → punishment repairs failed inscription → territorial system meets its external destruction.

### ch 6: The Barbarian Despotic Machine

- Basic transformation: the full body of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Socius|socius]] shifts from the earth → despot/State. The despot becomes the apparent source or [[Ideas/Concepts/Quasi-cause|quasi-cause]] of [[Ideas/Concepts/Social production|social production]], positioned above the social field. 
- The new machine is founded on [[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic new alliance|new alliance]] + [[Ideas/Concepts/Direct filiation|direct filiation]]:

- the despot establishes a new alliance with the people;
- he claims direct filiation with God/divinity;
- lateral alliances and extended filiations lose their determining position. This is why the despot is the paranoiac: everything is referred upward to his transcendent position. 

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Overcoding|Overcoding]]: the [[Ideas/Concepts/Primitive territorial machine|primitive territorial machine]] is not simply destroyed. Its communities, alliances, filiations, persons, and intrinsic codes persist, but become organs/bricks of the State machine. The old [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]] is “bricked over” by a second, higher inscription.  
	primitive code → preserved but subordinated → despotic overcode. 
- This means the State is simultaneously deterritorializing and reterritorializing: it uproots the earth as the ultimate full body and subjects the old territorialities to the abstract/transcendent unity of the State. 
- Asiatic production / megamachine: relatively autonomous rural communities continue doing the actual producing, while the State integrates them into a pyramid:  
	despot → bureaucracy/officials → village communities.  
	The State coordinates large works, appropriates surplus, and makes itself appear to be the source of the productive conditions it actually appropriates. 
- Finite debt → [[Ideas/Concepts/Infinite debt|infinite debt]].

- Primitive debt = mobile, [[Ideas/Concepts/Finite blocks of debt|finite blocks of debt]].
- Despotic debt = obligation to a transcendent creditor that can never finally be discharged.
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Taxes and State money|Taxes and State money]] help produce this relation; debt becomes almost a debt of existence itself. 

- The three syntheses literally reappear inside the State machine:

- Connective: the State connects the old alliances with the new alliance; concretely, productive machines such as hydraulic/mining works.
- Disjunctive: old filiations are redistributed/overflow into the despot’s direct filiation; its inscription-machines include accounting, writing, monuments.
- Conjunctive: the State distributes the appropriated product between the higher unity and subordinate communities; concretely, consumption/upkeep of the despot, court, and bureaucracy. 

- The despotic possibility was already prefigured inside the primitive machine. D&G say the death that arrives “from without” was also rising from within: the autonomy of [[Ideas/Concepts/Alliance|alliance]] groups, rankings, political/economic functions of alliance, and [[Ideas/Concepts/Surplus value of code|surplus value of code]] could already point toward caste and despotic organization. Primitive society therefore both contains the tendency toward the despot and actively wards it off. The actual State nevertheless arrives as a contingent break/leap, not as inevitable evolution. 
- The State shares one major fear with the primitive machine: decoded [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]]. It wants flows organized and monopolized rather than escaping its overcoding. 


Spine:  
earth → despot as full body → new alliance + direct filiation → primitive codes retained as bricks → [[Ideas/Concepts/Second inscription|second inscription]]/overcoding → State appropriates production and surplus → finite debt becomes infinite debt → all three syntheses reorganized around the transcendent unity of the despot.


### ch 7. Barbarian or Imperial Representation

- This section gives the representational/semiotic form of the [[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic machine|despotic machine]]. The political [[Ideas/Concepts/Overcoding|overcoding]] from §6 now appears as a transformation of incest, writing, signs, law, debt, and repression. 
- [[Ideas/Concepts/Royal incest|Royal incest]] expresses the two despotic operations:

- sister → [[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic new alliance|new alliance]]
- mother → [[Ideas/Concepts/Direct filiation|direct filiation]]
- The despot overcodes lateral alliances through the sister/new alliance and extended filiations through the mother/direct filiation. Whether he literally sleeps with his biological sister or mother is secondary; incest functions as the representation of despotic overcoding. 

- Incest now becomes possible.

- In the primitive regime, kinship names and actual persons never coincided in the way required for incest.
- Under [[Ideas/Concepts/Imperial representation|imperial representation]], signifier and signified become joined: sister and mother become determinate signifieds under the despotic signifier.
- This is a major migration of the elements of representation and begins preparing the conditions for [[Ideas/Concepts/Oedipus and familialization|Oedipus]]. 

- Transformation of the magic triangle

- Primitive: [[Ideas/Concepts/Voice–graphism–eye|voice–graphism–eye]] remain heterogeneous and polyvocal.
- Imperial: graphism aligns itself on the voice and becomes writing.
- Voice now dictates/decrees, graphism moves onto tablets/books/monuments, and the eye reads.
- The old triangle does not simply disappear: it survives as the base/bricks of a pyramid, with all three sides converging on the despot. 

- Transcendent object / despotic signifier

- Once graphism is subordinated to voice, a [[Ideas/Concepts/Detached transcendent object|detached transcendent object]] appears “above” the chain.
- The chain becomes linearized and subordinated to this eminent unity.
- Instead of the primitive polyvocal network, there is now a despotic signifier from which signs seem to flow.
- This is also where “What does it mean?” begins replacing the primitive question of use/function. 

- Signifier / signified

- The despot = signifier.
- sister + mother = signifieds of [[Ideas/Concepts/New alliance and direct filiation|new alliance and direct filiation]].
- Imperial representation therefore converts the old system of connotation into one of subordination/signification. 

- Law = overcoding / juridical form of [[Ideas/Concepts/Infinite debt|infinite debt]].

- Law does not initially function as protection against despotism; it is produced by the despotic machine.
- It gives juridical form to the infinite debt and makes the transcendent unity operative across the social field.
- So your intuition was basically right: the old inscriptions are subordinated to a higher [[Ideas/Concepts/Inscription|inscription]]/full body, but the crucial point is that overcoding itself is the essence of this law. 

- Cruelty → terror; punishment → vengeance.

- Primitive cruelty operated through visible action/reaction in the magic triangle.
- Imperial punishment becomes vengeance of the State/new alliance.
- Subjects are made passive while the power to react is elevated and concentrated above them.
- This produces [[Ideas/Concepts/Latency|latency]]: forces that could act outwardly are held back/internalized. 

- Vengeance / [[Ideas/Concepts/Ressentiment|ressentiment]] / infinite debt

- Above: the despot/Law possesses an effectively inexhaustible power of vengeance.
- Below: blocked reaction develops into ressentiment.
- Infinite social debt therefore begins producing an interior psychic form, which will matter enormously for later Oedipalization.

- Oedipus is beginning to migrate, but “this is still not Oedipus.”

- The despotic machine creates crucial formal conditions: transcendent object, signifier, law, infinite debt, [[Ideas/Concepts/Fixed signifieds|fixed signifieds]], biunivocal relations.
- But these still operate at the level of the State and sovereign, not yet as the private daddy–mommy–me familial complex. 


Spine:  
new alliance + direct filiation → royal incest expresses overcoding → graphism subordinated to voice → magic triangle becomes despotic pyramid → transcendent signifier + fixed signifieds → law/infinite debt → terror, vengeance, latency, ressentiment → Oedipal machinery begins to become possible, but is not yet the private Oedipus.

### ch. 8: The Urstaat

- [[Ideas/Concepts/Urstaat|Urstaat]] = abstract State-form, first realized in the [[Ideas/Concepts/Despotic machine|despotic machine]]; it can go latent and return under very different historical forms.
- Beginning ≠ origin: primitive societies may come first historically, while the Urstaat is the abstract State-form that later formations repeatedly reactivate. 
- Feudalism: not simply anti-State. Commodity production can actually reinforce feudalism, while the feudal State tries to contain decoded [[Ideas/Concepts/Flows|flows]] and reconstruct the Urstaat under new conditions; the despot returns as the absolute monarch. 
- Democracy: still bears the Urstaat within it—the despot becomes more dispersed, calculating, and “cold,” operating through counting/coding rather than straightforward imperial [[Ideas/Concepts/Overcoding|overcoding]]. 
- Over time the State becomes more concrete/immanent to class, property, money, and economic relations rather than standing purely above them as a transcendent unity. 
- Capitalism both breaks with and reuses the Urstaat: it replaces overcoding with an axiomatic of decoded flows, yet continually resurrects State-form as a pole of regulation and [[Ideas/Concepts/Reterritorialization|reterritorialization]]. 


Spine:  
Urstaat = recurring State-form → latent/returns through feudalism, monarchy, democracy, etc. → becomes increasingly immanent → capitalism breaks with it while continually reactivating it.

### ch 9: The Civilized Capitalist Machine

Origin: Capitalism emerges through the contingent conjunction of decoded money-capital and deterritorialized labor. Decoded flows existed previously, but their conjunction produces capitalism.

Three social machines: Primitive = connection; Despotic = disjunction; Capitalist = conjunction (each privileges a synthesis).

Capital as socius: Industrial capital directly appropriates production, becoming the new full body/quasi-cause. Production appears to emanate from capital itself.

New filiation: Capital becomes filiative (money begets money), while commercial and financial capital assume functions of alliance.

Surplus value of flux: Surplus value of code gives way to differential relations of decoded flows. Money has two asymmetrical functions: payment (wages/purchasing power) and financing (credit/investment).

Immanent limits: Capitalism continually displaces and reproduces its internal limits on an expanding scale. Crises become mechanisms of its reproduction.

Machinic surplus value: Technology, science, information, and automation generate surplus value, but innovations remain subordinated to the capitalist axiomatic.

Antiproduction: The State, military, bureaucracy, etc. operate within production, regulating it and absorbing surplus value. Capitalism produces lack amid abundance.

Desire: Capitalism is directly invested by desire through flows of money, labor, credit, and consumption—not merely through ideological deception.

Capitalism and schizophrenia: Capitalism decodes and deterritorializes flows while continually recapturing them. Schizophrenia is its absolute limit, which capitalism approaches but continually displaces.

Central movement: Decoding → conjunction → capital as socius → surplus value of flux → expanding immanent limits → deterritorialization and recapture.

### §10. Capitalist Representation

1. Capitalist signs and representation

- Despotic representation subordinates signs to a transcendent signifier (voice, writing, law, despot). Capitalist representation increasingly operates through figures or schizzes: nonsignifying points-signs and breaks-flows that possess no fixed identity independently of their connections.
- Hjelmslev replaces the hierarchy of signifier/signified with a reciprocal determination of content/expression. Signs function through immanent relations rather than deriving their meaning from a transcendent unity.
- Lyotard recognizes the figural beneath signification, where words, images, and intensities escape fixed identities. D&G criticize him for ultimately returning desire to fantasy, lack, and castration.
- Capitalist representation therefore favors information, calculation, and nonsignifying operations over the despotic regime of writing and signification. Computers, electronic signals, and monetary operations exemplify this movement.

1. Capitalism versus schizophrenia

- Both capitalism and schizophrenia involve decoded, deterritorialized flows, but their regimes differ.

- Capitalism: Decodes flows while simultaneously binding them within an axiomatic. It is the relative limit of previous social formations.
- Schizophrenia as process: Unbound flows escaping capitalist capture, passing onto the BwO. It constitutes capitalism’s absolute or exterior limit.

- Capitalism both produces and represses schizophrenic processes because unrestricted decoding would threaten the capitalist machine itself.
- Decoding and axiomatization occur simultaneously, not as two successive stages. Capitalism’s flows are never simply released and subsequently captured.

1. Codes versus the capitalist axiomatic

Codes establish relations between already qualified flows through kinship, status, territory, belief, law, etc. Their relations are generally indirect, qualitative, limited, and extraeconomic.

The axiomatic differs through four characteristics:

- Abstract quantitative equivalence: Money operates as a general equivalent, rendering qualitatively different flows economically commensurable. Unlike finite coded exchanges, the circulation of capital has no intrinsic endpoint.
- Direct differential relations: Capitalism establishes direct relations between decoded flows, particularly labor and capital. Their specifically capitalist qualities emerge through their conjunction, rather than existing beforehand as coded identities.
- Indefinite expansion: The axiomatic can continually add axioms, displacing its internal limits and accommodating new flows without abolishing capital’s fundamental organization.
- No necessary inscription upon bodies: Unlike primitive marking and despotic overcoding, capitalism operates through abstract quantities, economic functions, and technical-administrative mechanisms. Individuals increasingly become private persons defined economically through labor capacity or capital.

The axiomatic is thus not simply a filter imposed upon independently existing flows. It establishes the differential relations through which those flows acquire specifically capitalist functions.

1. The three aspects of the capitalist axiomatic

D&G identify three interrelated aspects:

- Differential relations: Decoded flows of labor and capital enter relations through which surplus value is produced.
- Expansion of internal limits: Capital encounters barriers only to displace and reproduce them on wider scales.
- Antiproduction within production: The absorption and realization of surplus value require regulatory and antipproductive mechanisms operating inside the productive system.

These are the fundamental economic operations of capitalist immanence.

Important: The axiomatic is not identical to a particular law, government, or technological apparatus. D&G identify it with capital’s immanent economic relations themselves; the State and other institutions regulate and execute it.

1. The forms and functions of capital

Continuing §9’s distinction:

- Industrial capital: The productive basis of capitalism, through which capital directly appropriates production and becomes filiative (value engendering surplus value).
- Commercial/merchant capital: Organizes circulation and exchange. Before capitalism, merchants could remain allied with preexisting modes of production.
- Financial capital: Organizes financing and credit, enabling the command and reorganization of production.

Capital is industrial in its productive basis, but this industrial production functions through commercial and financial relations.

This also explains the asymmetry between money as payment (wages, purchasing power) and money as financing (credit and the economic power to organize production). Their differential relation is central to the axiomatic.

1. Technical machines and the social machine

- Capitalism does not replace the social machine with merely technical machines.
- Technical machines become components of constant capital, while human labor increasingly becomes adjacent to them.
- The axiomatic still requires social decision-making, bureaucracy, technocracy, and administration. It cannot operate as a purely automatic technical system.
- People are therefore not primarily subordinated to individual machines but to the capitalist social machine organizing them.

1. The capitalist State and its forms

- The despotic State imposed a transcendent overcoding upon social production. The capitalist State emerges within the conjunction of decoded flows and becomes their immanent regulator.
- The State regulates wages, production, credit, accumulation, crises, and the absorption of surplus value.
- D&G explicitly reject the idea of historically independent, unrestricted laissez-faire capitalism: State intervention has accompanied capitalist development from its inception.
- Adding axioms: When capitalism encounters a new demand or obstacle, it can incorporate it by modifying its operations without fundamentally changing its regime. Recognition of unions and organized labor is their central example.

Capitalist and socialist State formations:

- Bourgeois capitalism: A relatively flexible, expansive axiomatic capable of incorporating demands and displacing limits.
- State socialism: Risks establishing a more rigid axiomatic under a bureaucracy or technocracy, reproducing capitalist functions through State organization.
- Fascism: An especially intense attempt at political and economic reterritorialization through authoritarian revival.

D&G argue that planning versus markets is not the decisive distinction: capitalist States plan, while socialist States can preserve market operations.

1. Class and capitalist subjection

- From the standpoint of the capitalist axiomatic, the bourgeoisie is the only class in the strict theoretical sense: the class constituted through the destruction of earlier castes, statuses, and codes.
- This does not deny actual exploitation or class conflict. Rather, the capitalist machine tends to integrate workers and capitalists into the same expansive system of accumulation.
- Even the capitalist is subordinated to capital’s reproduction: the apparent master is himself a servant of the machine. Accumulation replaces personal enjoyment as the overriding end.
- The proletariat becomes a revolutionary class through praxis: political organization establishes a class bipolarity that capitalism’s own axiomatic tends to absorb.
- But revolutionary organization risks producing a new State apparatus and bureaucracy, restoring domination rather than escaping it.
- The deeper opposition is therefore between flows captured by the capitalist class axiomatic and flows that escape it.

1. Desire versus interest

- Class interest operates at a preconscious, molar level: objective positions, economic interests, political organization, and their representation.
- Desire operates through unconscious libidinal investments of the social field and groups.
- Interests can be misunderstood or betrayed; desire cannot simply be deceived. People may genuinely desire fascism, repression, or their own subordination.
- Revolutionary politics therefore cannot consist merely in enlightening people about their objective interests.
- Following Reich and Sartre, D&G distinguish unconscious group desire from preconscious class interest, locating the revolutionary problem in the relation between them.

1. Deterritorialization and reterritorialization

- Capitalism continually dissolves inherited identities, codes, and territorial attachments while generating new territorialities that stabilize its flows.
- These neoarchaisms include nationalism, familialism, local identities, minority enclaves, gangs, political parties, and revived traditional forms.
- Such archaisms are not merely historical residues: they perform contemporary functions within capitalism and may acquire reactionary or revolutionary investments.
- Reterritorialization also occurs economically: the State regulates financial flows and purchasing power, while global deterritorialization generates new political and economic territorial formations.
- D&G’s discussion of political economy follows the same movement: mercantilism ties wealth to money, physiocracy privileges agriculture and land, and Adam Smith discovers abstract labor as wealth’s productive essence but reterritorializes it through private property.

Deterritorialization and reterritorialization are simultaneous aspects of one process. Capitalism develops production beyond established limits, but only to reproduce its own conditions and limits on a wider scale.

1. The two poles of capitalist society

Capitalism perpetually oscillates between two opposing tendencies:

- Paranoiac/despotic pole: Revival of the Urstaat, authority, overcoding, nationalism, dictatorship, hierarchy, and fixed identities.
- Schizophrenic/revolutionary pole: Unbound flows, molecular movements, escapes from the axiomatic, and deterritorialization beyond capitalist regulation.

Both capitalism and State socialism are susceptible to these opposing investments.

The modern State simultaneously breaks with the Urstaat and resurrects it: it no longer overcodes as the ancient despot did, but employs despotic forms for reterritorializing the flows capitalism has decoded.

Capitalism consequently oscillates between reviving despotic authority and approaching the schizophrenic limit that threatens its dissolution.

### §11. Oedipus at Last

- Privatization of the family: In primitive and despotic societies, familial alliance and filiation directly organize social reproduction. Under capitalism, economic reproduction becomes autonomous, and the family is relegated to the private sphere of human reproduction.

- Two orders of images:

- Social persons (first order): The capitalist, worker, etc., produced as functions of economic flows.
- Private persons (second order): Father, mother, child—simulacra onto which the entire social field is projected.
- Oedipalization: Social investments are flattened onto the familial triangle (daddy–mommy–me). The family becomes an application of the capitalist axiomatic rather than an organizer of social production.

- Oedipus as internalized limit: Capitalism continually approaches its schizophrenic limit but displaces it inward through familial reterritorialization. Desire’s investment of the social field is reduced to the private family, restricting its lines of escape.

- Oedipus across the three machines:

- Primitive: Empty, unoccupied limit.
- Despotic: Symbolically occupied limit (imperial incest, transcendent Law).
- Capitalist: Imaginary, inhabited, and lived limit (the privatized Oedipal family).
- Oedipus is therefore a historical product, not a universal foundation of desire.

- The familial triangle: The mother becomes a simulacrum of territoriality; the father, of despotic Law; the child, a divided and castrated ego. Earlier social forms are recapitulated within private familial representation.

- Bad conscience and repression: Oedipus internalizes infinite debt, guilt, ressentiment, and repression. The private subject becomes responsible for desires whose actual investments extend across the social field.

- Freud and Adam Smith: Both discover an abstract subjective essence—Smith: labor/production; Freud: libido/desire—but subsequently reterritorialize their discoveries. Smith subordinates production to private property; Freud subordinates desire to the family.

- Psychoanalysis: Does not invent Oedipus but consolidates it, transforming social investments into familial fantasies and internalizing repression through guilt, castration, and the analyst’s authority.

- Schizoanalysis: Reverses this reduction by discovering the social investments and collective agents of desire beneath familial images. It replaces the theater of Oedipal representation with desiring-production.

Central movement: Capitalist privatization of the family → social images projected onto private familial simulacra → Oedipus as an internalized limit → psychoanalytic reterritorialization of desire → schizoanalysis as the recovery of desire’s social and machinic production.

## Part 4: Introduction to Schizoanalysis 

### §1. The Social Field — The Body Without Organs

- BwO as cosmic egg: A giant molecule swarming with organisms, machines, and heterogeneous productions. It is not empty or exclusively molecular; it contains two distinct sides.
- Two sides of the BwO:

- Microscopic: Mass phenomena and their paranoiac investments, organized into larger unities (molar).
- Submicroscopic: Molecular phenomena and their schizophrenic investments, operating through multiplicities and flows.

- BwO as frontier: The BwO is the pivot between molar/paranoiac and molecular/schizophrenic investments. Their division occurs on the BwO.
- Socius ≠ projection of the BwO: The socius (earth, despot, capital-money) consists of clothed full bodies, whereas the BwO is the naked full body.
- Limit, not origin: The BwO exists at the limit of the socius, as its tangent of deterritorialization and ultimate residue, rather than as its primordial foundation.
- Historical qualification: The BwO haunts every form of socius, but paranoia and schizophrenia as opposing poles of social investment acquire their specific historical determination under capitalism. They are not phenomena that exist exclusively within capitalism.

Key formulation:

The socius is not a projection of the body without organs; rather, the body without organs is the limit of the socius.

Central distinction: The socius organizes and inscribes production upon a full body; the BwO marks its limit of deterritorialization, where molar/paranoiac and molecular/schizophrenic investments diverge.

### §2. The Molecular Unconscious

- Literal machinism: Desiring-machines are not metaphors. Organisms are machines, and machines are organs; both participate in the same productive reality. Following Samuel Butler, D&G reject the distinction between vitalism and mechanism.

- Two states of machines:

- Molecular: Desiring-machines, whose functioning and formation are inseparable. They produce through connections, breaks, flows, and partial objects.
- Molar: Organic, technical, and social machines organized into statistical aggregates, stable structures, and identities.
- These are not different machines in nature, but different regimes of the same machines. Molar formations are real organizations of molecular production, not its independent foundation.

- Molecular functionalism: Only at the molecular level do production, product, assembly, and functioning coincide. Desiring-machines have no intrinsic meaning, intention, or purpose; they simply function and produce.

- The three syntheses: Molecular production operates through connections, inclusive disjunctions, and nomadic conjunctions. Molar organization restricts these into fixed connections, exclusive disjunctions, and determinate identities. The subject remains a residual product of the syntheses, not their origin.

- The molecular unconscious: D&G draw on molecular biology (Monod) and Szondi to describe an unconscious constituted by machinic connections rather than familial representations. The unconscious continually produces and reproduces itself through its own arrangements.

- Libido: The specific energy of desiring-machines. Its transformations into Numen and Voluptas are not desexualizations or sublimations. Libido directly invests biological, political, economic, and social fields without first passing through the family.

- Gigantism and dwarfism of desire:

- Gigantism: Sexuality invests entire social fields, populations, institutions, and worlds.
- Dwarfism: Sexuality simultaneously operates through microscopic desiring-machines and molecular multiplicities.
- These are two inseparable dimensions of the same libido. Desire does not primarily take isolated persons as its objects; persons are intersections of larger libidinal flows.

- Sexuality and repression: Freud’s restriction of sexuality to persons and families reverses the actual movement of desire. Sexuality is originally expansive; its confinement to the couple, family, and individual is a secondary restriction.

- Human versus nonhuman sex:

- Human sexuality: Molar, anthropomorphic representation organized around the statistical categories of male and female.
- Nonhuman sexuality: The molecular desiring-machines, their arrangements, multiplicities, and syntheses that produce sexuality prior to its organization into fixed categories.
- Following Marx, the fundamental distinction is not between man and woman but between human and nonhuman sex.

- Critique of psychoanalytic sexuality:

- Freud/Lacan: The phallic organization of sexuality through absence, lack, and castration.
- Melanie Klein: A positive determination of feminine sexuality that nevertheless retains molar sexual categories.
- Both remain trapped in anthropomorphic representation, overlooking molecular multiplicities and the productive syntheses of desire.

- The n sexes: The molecular unconscious knows neither a single privileged sex nor an exhaustive division into two sexes. Sexuality consists of innumerable machinic combinations and potential conjunctions. Sexual identity is a molar organization of a prior molecular multiplicity, not the foundation of desire.

Central argument: The unconscious is a molecular field of desiring-machines whose production precedes molar organization, fixed identities, and familial representation. Libido directly invests the social field, and sexuality is fundamentally multiple rather than organized around lack or sexual binaries.

Central movement: Literal machinism → molecular/molar distinction → productive syntheses → libido’s direct social investment → gigantism/dwarfism → nonhuman sexuality → n sexes.

### §3. Psychoanalysis and Capitalism

- Production vs. representation: The unconscious is a factory of desiring-production, not a theater of representations. Psychoanalysis reduces productive desire to familial fantasies, symbols, and interpretations.
- Capitalism and psychoanalysis: Both discover productive forces only to recapture them. Capitalism deterritorializes production and reterritorializes it through private property and its axiomatic; psychoanalysis discovers libido but confines it to Oedipus and the family.
- Structuralism and lack: Even when psychoanalysis moves beyond familial images toward symbolic structures, it continues subordinating desire to lack, castration, and the signifier.
- Negative task of schizoanalysis: Destroy Oedipal representations and their associated constructs—guilt, castration, familialism, and the sovereign ego. These are imposed restrictions, not the underlying truth of desire.
- Beyond representation: Beneath these constructs lie desiring-machines, flows, breaks, and machinic indices of deterritorialization. Schizoanalysis seeks to uncover their actual functioning rather than interpret their supposed meanings.
- Toward the positive task: Destruction is preparatory, clearing the way for new connections, productive arrangements, and the possibility of a new earth.

Central movement: Destruction of Oedipal representation → liberation from imposed interpretations → investigation of desiring-production.

Central takeaway: The negative task of schizoanalysis is to dismantle the representational structures that conceal and repress the productive unconscious, preparing the way for its positive investigation.

### §4. The First Positive Task of Schizoanalysis

1. The first positive task: discovering desiring-machines

- Schizoanalysis is functional, not interpretative. Its task is to discover what a subject’s desiring-machines are, how they form, what they connect to, what flows they produce and interrupt, and how their operations produce a residual subject.
- The schizoanalyst is a mechanic: Rather than asking what dreams, fantasies, or behaviors mean, schizoanalysis investigates how their underlying molecular machinery actually functions.
- Machinic indices: Dreams, fantasies, technical machines, social arrangements, and personal experiences can provide clues to desiring-machines, but they are not necessarily the molecular machines themselves. Analysis must move beyond these molar representations.

1. Partial objects and molecular multiplicity

- Partial objects: The working parts of desiring-machines. They are mutually independent, really distinct, and dispersed—not fragments of a lost organism or components requiring a unified whole.
- Molecular production: Partial objects connect indirectly through flows and breaks. One machine emits a flow that another interrupts, while itself producing a flow for another machine.
- No prior unity: Desiring-machines operate without a preexisting subject, organism, identity, or organizing structure. Their connections produce temporary arrangements rather than expressing an underlying whole.
- Passive syntheses:

- Connective: Partial objects produce and interrupt flows.
- Disjunctive: Flows overlap and diverge through inclusive disjunctions, permitting different functions and arrangements to coexist.
- Conjunctive: Flows interfere, intensities emerge, and a residual subject is produced through nomadic becomings.

1. The BwO and the desiring-machines

- Co-production: The BwO is produced alongside partial objects through the connective synthesis. It is a whole added alongside the parts, not a totality that unifies them.
- Repulsion and attraction:

- Paranoiac machine: The BwO repels organs-partial objects, establishing their opposition.
- Miraculating machine: Organs become attached to the BwO; production appears to emanate from its surface, although the BwO is not itself the productive source.
- These movements coexist. Repulsion conditions functioning; attraction is the functioning itself. Desiring-machines function through their interruptions and breakdowns.

- The real opposition is to the organism: The BwO and partial objects are not fundamentally opposed. Both resist the molar organization of organs into a fixed, unified organism.
- Spinozist immanence: The BwO is immanent substance or matter at intensity zero; partial objects are its really distinct intensive powers or attributes. They are distinct but constitute one immanent multiplicity.
- Molecular chain: The recording chain on the BwO connects partial objects through included disjunctions. Unlike social codes or the capitalist axiomatic, its molecular operation is one of decoding and deterritorialization, allowing flows to escape fixed configurations.

1. Death, catatonia, and the functioning of desire

- BwO as the model of death: Catatonic schizophrenia provides the model of death through the BwO’s repulsion of organs and its zero degree of intensity.
- Death desires: D&G reject an independent death drive qualitatively opposed to life. Death is not an object that desire seeks; rather, death and life are two coexisting kinds of parts within desiring-machines:

- Death: The immobile motor/BwO, associated with repulsion and zero intensity.
- Life: The working parts/partial objects, associated with productive activity.
- These are not two independent desires, but heterogeneous dimensions of one machinery.

- Model versus experience of death:

- Model: Zero intensity, the nonproductive condition associated with the BwO.
- Experience: Every becoming or passage through intensities involves the zero intensity from which intensities emerge and toward which they can return.
- The desiring-machine continually converts the model into the experience of death through its functioning.

- Schizophrenizing death: Death is experienced immanently through intensive transformations and the nomadic subject’s becomings. This differs from the actual, final death of the individual. Each return to zero can condition further machinic productions and new becomings.

1. Freud’s death instinct and capitalist repression

- Critique of Freud: Freud separates Eros and Thanatos into opposed drives, establishing death as a transcendent principle. D&G reject this dualism because it obscures the machinery’s energetic conversions.
- Immanent death versus transcendent death: For D&G, death has both a model and an experience within the unconscious. It therefore does not require an independent, transcendent death instinct.
- Psychoanalysis and capitalism: Both subordinate productive forces to restrictive representations:

- Labor is alienated through private property.
- Libido is alienated through the privatized family and Oedipus.

- Capitalism’s death instinct: Capitalism integrates antiproduction throughout production. The historically produced death instinct expresses this mortification of desire, as productive forces become subordinated to repression, guilt, and the reproduction of capital.
- Central paradox: Capitalism reveals the identity in nature of social and desiring-production more clearly than previous societies, yet imposes an especially severe difference in regime between them. Its decoded flows coexist with an unprecedented apparatus of repression.

1. The machinery and its three components

|   |   |   |   |
|---|---|---|---|
||Part|Energy|Synthesis|
|Production|Working parts / partial objects|Libido|Connective — flows and breaks|
|Recording|Immobile motor / BwO|Numen|Disjunctive — singularities and chains|
|Consumption-consummation|Adjacent / residual subject|Voluptas|Conjunctive — intensities and becomings|

These are coexisting aspects of desiring-production, not independent mechanisms or chronological stages.

1. Primal repression and the practical task

- Primal repression: The BwO’s repulsion of partial objects creates an internal condition within desiring-production upon which social and psychic repression can subsequently operate.
- Repression exploits this condition: Molar structures, Oedipal representations, and social institutions seize upon primal repression, restricting molecular production and imposing fixed identities or meanings.
- Machinic conversion: The first positive task seeks to transform repulsion from an occasion for repression into a condition of functioning. It follows the machines through attraction, breakdowns, and the production of new intensities, allowing them to function again.
- Negative and positive tasks are simultaneous: Destroying restrictive representations and discovering desiring-machines are not separate stages. Each enables the other.
- Withdrawal as revolutionary investment: A line of flight is not necessarily an abandonment of the social field; it can be a direct libidinal investment of that field. Withdrawal from established codes, identities, and forms of power can become revolutionary insofar as it produces new connections and disrupts existing social arrangements

Central argument: Schizoanalysis investigates the molecular functioning of desiring-machines rather than interpreting their representations. It discovers mutually independent partial objects, their passive syntheses, the BwO, and the residual subject produced through their operations. Even repulsion, breakdown, and death belong to this machinery rather than constituting independent drives.

Central movement: Destruction of representation → discovery of partial objects → three passive syntheses → BwO and molecular chain → model and experience of death → critique of Freud’s death instinct → machinic conversion of primal repression.

Central takeaway: The first positive task is not to discover what desire means or what the subject really wants, but to determine how desire produces, how its machines are restricted, and how they can function through new connections and intensities.


### §5: The Second Positive Task of Schizoanalysis


1. The second positive task: discovering the social investments of desire

- First positive task: Discover the molecular desiring-machines, their connections, breaks, syntheses, and functioning.
- Second positive task: Determine how unconscious desire invests the social field, distinguishing its reactionary and revolutionary investments.
- Identity in nature, difference in regime: Desiring-production and social production are not separate processes. Molecular desiring-machines compose and inhabit molar social machines; social machines organize these same productive forces under historically specific conditions.
- Two directions of subordination:

- Paranoiac: Molecular desiring-production is subordinated to molar aggregates, fixed organizations, and established forms of power.
- Schizorevolutionary: Molecular multiplicities subordinate and transform molar arrangements, opening social production to new connections.

- Neither pole exists outside society. The distinction is not simply between individual desire and social organization, since even molecular production directly invests the social field.

Central problem: How can desire transform the social machines it constitutes without becoming subordinated to their repressive organization?

1. The four theses of schizoanalysis

First thesis — Every unconscious investment is social.

- Desire always invests a sociohistorical field, never an exclusively private or familial domain.
- Molecular multiplicities constitute molar formations through processes of selection, accumulation, and organization.
- Gregariousness: The formation of large social aggregates through the selection and organization of molecular forces. These aggregates do not precede the selection that constitutes them.
- Production of lack: Molar organizations establish apparent wholes against which partial objects appear deficient. Lack is socially organized rather than original to desire.
- Every social formation organizes desire through a particular socius: the earth, the despot, or capital.

Second thesis — Unconscious libidinal investment differs from preconscious investment of class or interest.

- Preconscious interest: Investment in recognizable social aims, economic positions, political objectives, and institutional arrangements.
- Unconscious libidinal investment: Desire’s direct investment in the productive forces, flows, codes, and forms of sovereignty constituting the social field.
- These investments may coincide or contradict one another.
- Someone can consciously support revolution while unconsciously investing in hierarchy, authority, and repression.
- Desire is part of the infrastructure, not merely an ideological representation of underlying economic interests.
- Consequently, ideological mystification alone cannot explain why people desire their own repression.

Third thesis — Social libidinal investments are primary in relation to familial investments.

- Desire directly invests the social field; familial investments are secondary determinations of this broader investment.
- Parents may function as stimuli or points of application, but the social field organizes the intensities that familial relations express.
- Oedipus reduces these wider investments to the familial triangle, mistaking their derivative representation for their productive origin.
- Consequently, the unconscious cannot be adequately understood through familial interpretation.

Fourth thesis — Social libidinal investment has two poles.

- Paranoiac-reactionary: Desire invests fixed identities, hierarchical aggregates, segregation, authority, and the repression of its own molecular production.
- Schizoid-revolutionary: Desire invests lines of flight, inclusive disjunctions, molecular multiplicities, and the transformation of established social arrangements.
- These poles coexist and fluctuate within individuals, groups, and institutions; they are not two separate kinds of people.
- Their decisive distinction concerns which regime of production subordinates the other.

1. Desire, interest, and the problem of revolution

- Political revolution is not necessarily libidinal revolution.

- A preconscious revolutionary break establishes new institutions, goals, and distributions of power.
- An unconscious revolutionary break transforms the relation between desire and the social organization that governs it.

- A revolution can replace one socius with another while preserving the subordination of desiring-production.
- Thus, reactionary libidinal investments can persist beneath revolutionary political objectives.
- Desiring one’s repression: Desire can directly invest the machinery that oppresses it, independent of whether that investment serves recognizable interests.
- The revolutionary problem is therefore not simply to establish new institutions, but to prevent these institutions from reproducing the previous regime of desire.

1. Subject-groups and subjugated groups

Subjugated groups

- Subordinate desire to the preservation of the group, its hierarchy, identity, and authority.
- Organize themselves through fixed structures, group narcissism, repression, and a collective superego.
- Can possess revolutionary political objectives while remaining reactionary at the level of unconscious investment.

Subject-groups

- Subordinate established social organizations to the productive movement of desire.
- Permit transversal connections and transformations that exceed fixed hierarchies.
- Create provisional, transformable arrangements rather than treating their organization as permanent.
- Remain open to further ruptures and experimentation.

Their relationship is reversible: Subject-groups can become subjugated groups through reterritorialization, while new subject-groups can emerge through ruptures within existing organizations. The distinction concerns the functioning of a group, not its declared ideology. 

1. Withdrawal, lines of flight, and revolutionary investment

- A line of flight is not necessarily withdrawal from the social field. Desire cannot escape social investment because desire is already social.
- The schizoid process exceeds individual withdrawal: it makes established social arrangements themselves escape, opening productive connections beyond their restrictions.
- Withdrawal is not inherently revolutionary. Its revolutionary character depends on whether it transforms social investments rather than merely establishing personal isolation or another restrictive territory.
- The clinical schizophrenic must therefore be distinguished from the schizophrenic process. The former is not automatically revolutionary; the latter designates a potential for revolutionary transformation.

Central distinction: Escaping an organization differs from making the organization itself undergo a productive transformation.

1. Sexuality and the critique of psychoanalysis

- Sexuality extends throughout the social field. Libido directly invests economic, political, historical, and collective processes without requiring desexualization or sublimation.
- Sexual desire is not fundamentally organized around fixed persons, identities, objects, or familial roles.
- Psychoanalysis restricts sexuality through Oedipal representations, identification, castration, and the reduction of desire to familial figures.
- Sexual revolution: Not merely the liberation of new sexual objects, aims, or identities, but a transformation of the machinic arrangements through which sexuality functions.
- Love and sexual relations can exhibit reactionary or revolutionary investments according to how their flows are organized.
- Nonfigurative sexuality: Sexuality understood through connections, vibrations, intensities, and flows rather than predetermined identities or representations.

1. Psychiatry, antipsychiatry, and schizophrenia

- Psychoanalysis continually reintroduces familial explanations into processes whose determinants exceed the family.
- Even antipsychiatry can remain familialist when it explains schizophrenia through pathological family relationships or seeks to restore an authentic ego.
- Schizophrenia as process: A movement of decoded flows exceeding established organizations.
- Schizophrenia as clinical condition: May involve the interruption, blockage, or destructive arrest of this process.
- Schizoanalysis does not idealize psychiatric suffering or advocate producing clinical schizophrenia.
- Its task is to investigate how desiring-production becomes blocked and how its productive functioning might be restored.

1. Art, science, and experimentation

- Art and science possess revolutionary potential through their production of new connections, not necessarily through their explicit representations or political messages.
- Art can deterritorialize established codes of representation, producing nonfigurative relations, unfamiliar intensities, and new arrangements of experience.
- Science can similarly experiment with processes and relations exceeding established explanatory frameworks.
- Both can establish lines of flight within the social field.
- However, neither is inherently revolutionary: both can be subordinated to existing institutions, economic arrangements, and forms of power.
- Experimentation is productive rather than interpretative. Its significance concerns what new relations and possibilities are generated, not simply what something represents.

1. Capitalism and revolutionary possibility

- Capitalism continuously decodes and deterritorializes flows, but recombines them through its axiomatic and produces corresponding reterritorializations.
- Consequently, deterritorialization alone is not revolutionary: capitalism itself depends upon it.
- Capitalism can absorb opposition by expanding its axiomatic, introducing new arrangements while preserving its fundamental relations of production.
- A revolutionary break must therefore exceed the simple replacement or reform of established institutions.
- Revolutionary ruptures: Material conditions prepare the possibility of revolution, but D&G insist that the event of rupture cannot be adequately reduced to an established sequence of causes, interests, and goals.
- The revolutionary potential of desire lies in its capacity to produce unexpected transformations of the social field.

1. The practical and political task of schizoanalysis

- Schizoanalysis does not prescribe a universal revolutionary organization or political program.
- It investigates the molecular machinery underlying social arrangements and distinguishes their unconscious investments.
- Its question is not merely whether a society satisfies particular interests, but what place it permits desiring-production, what connections it enables, and whether its organization can be transformed by the molecular forces composing it.
- The destructive and positive tasks operate simultaneously:

- Destructive: Undo repressive representations, Oedipal restrictions, and transcendent organizations of desire.
- First positive: Discover the actual functioning of molecular desiring-machines.
- Second positive: Discover and transform the social investments of these machines, opening possibilities for revolutionary production.

Central argument: Political transformation requires more than reorganizing established social institutions. It also requires transforming the unconscious investments through which desire constitutes and sustains those institutions.

Central distinction: A change in political interests or institutions does not necessarily constitute a revolutionary transformation of desire.

Central takeaway: Schizoanalysis investigates how social machines are constituted by desire, how these arrangements subordinate or enable desiring-production, and how revolutionary experimentation might transform their functioning without merely reproducing new forms of repression.
